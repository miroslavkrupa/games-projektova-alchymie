# Úvodní obrázek: Ruda Gantt a Bára Backlogová u kotle praktik

Zadání pro ChatGPT, stejný postup jako u grafiky ve hře *Termín byl včera*. Obrázek nahradí
`img/alchymie.webp` na úvodní obrazovce a v náhledu pro sdílení (`img/og.jpg`).

## Postup

1. Nová konverzace v ChatGPT. Nahraj předlohy z repozitáře `games-termin-byl-vcera`:
   - `docs/styl/vzorky/ruda-list_v03.png` (Ruda Gantt)
   - `docs/styl/vzorky/bara-list_v02.png` (Bára Backlogová)
2. Vlož zadání níže.
3. Ulož výsledek jako `navrh-obrazku/uvod_v01.png` (další pokus `_v02` …). Pozadí má být
   **průhledné**: postavy pak stojí přímo na kartě a sednou do světlého i tmavého režimu.
   Obrázek pak zhodnotím, převedu do WebP s průhledností, vložím do hry (bez rámečku) a podle
   něj přegeneruju náhled pro sdílení, kde ho podložím krémovým papírem.

## Zadání

```
Draw a wide illustration for the title screen of an educational game about project management called "Project Alchemy".
Use the two characters from the uploaded reference sheets exactly as they are drawn there: the same faces, hair, clothes, proportions and yellow socks.
Ruda, the tall thin man with messy dark hair, a white shirt with rolled-up sleeves and a red striped tie, stands on the left. He stands for careful planning: his wooden clipboard with a Gantt chart is tucked under one arm and with the other hand he stirs the cauldron with a long wooden paddle, looking very sure of his plan.
Bára, the woman with short red-brown hair, round glasses, a red shirt and faded teal denim overalls, stands on the right. She stands for agile iteration: she holds her tablet in one hand and with the other she drops a few small colourful sticky notes into the cauldron, with a slightly sceptical but friendly smile at Ruda.
Between them is a big old round iron cauldron on short legs over a small fire, with a strip of yellow-and-black safety tape wrapped around its rim.
The steam rising from the cauldron splits in two: on Ruda's side it forms neat ochre Gantt chart bars and small milestone diamonds, on Bára's side it forms faded-teal circular iteration arrows and fluttering sticky notes. High above the middle of the cauldron the two streams mix and form one glowing green check mark.
A few practice ingredients lie on the ground around the cauldron: a rolled-up plan, a small traffic light, a stack of index cards and a stopwatch.
Style: match the drawing style of the reference sheets: classic point-and-click comedy adventure caricature with exaggerated cartoon proportions, bold dark-brown ink outlines that are thicker on the outer contours, soft hand-painted watercolor washes with visible paper texture, a warm muted palette of ochre, rust and faded teal, and safety yellow only as an accent on the most important things.
Draw the steam shapes, the fire and the sticky notes as solid, fully opaque painted shapes with outlines, not as transparent mist or glow.
Transparent background: only the characters, the cauldron with its fire, the steam shapes and the ingredients. No paper, no background color, no glow, no shadow on the ground, no room, no scenery, no frame, no border.
Landscape 3:2 (1536 × 1024 pixels). Both characters and the whole cauldron are fully visible, with some empty space around them.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo.
```

## Opravné věty (když se něco nepovede)

- Postavy se nepodobají předlohám:
  `Redraw the same scene, but make Ruda and Bára look exactly like on the reference sheets: the same face, nose, hair, glasses and clothes. Keep everything else.`
- Objeví se text nebo písmena:
  `Keep the image exactly the same, only remove all text, letters and numbers, including those in the Gantt chart and on the sticky notes.`
- Pozadí není průhledné (papír, barva, záře nebo stín pod postavami):
  `Keep the image exactly the same, but make the background fully transparent. Remove the paper, the glow and the shadow on the ground.`
- Pára nebo oheň jsou poloprůhledné a rozpité:
  `Keep the image exactly the same, but paint the steam shapes and the fire as solid opaque shapes with dark-brown outlines.`
- Postavy jsou uříznuté:
  `Zoom out a little so that both characters, from their hair to their shoes, and the whole cauldron fit in the image with some empty space around them.`
